Frequently asked questions

Is shipping included?

Standard domestic shipping included for mainland Spain. For islands/international, please email us before purchasing.

How do I request the dedication?

Specify the exact name in your email and, if you wish, a brief note (birthday, gift, etc.). If it’s a surprise, we’ll take care.

How long will it take to arrive?

Preparation 3–5 business days + carrier transit. We’ll notify you when it ships with a tracking number when applicable.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We can ship outside Spain, though costs and delivery times vary by country. Email us before making the transfer and we’ll confirm the shipping surcharge.

Can I return a dedicated book?

No. Dedicated copies are personalised for each reader and, for legal and logistical reasons, cannot be returned once signed. Only in case of print defect or shipping damage can a replacement be arranged.

When will I receive my order confirmation?

Once we receive your transfer and your email with details, we’ll send confirmation within a maximum of 24 business hours.
